Acme Robotics — Series C Pitch Deck (Confidential)

The Problem
Third-party logistics providers are struggling with rising labor costs and
fulfillment SLAs. Existing warehouse automation is rigid, expensive to
install, and slow to redeploy.

Our Solution
Acme Robotics builds autonomous warehouse robotics systems for third-party
logistics providers. Our fleet of mobile manipulation robots can be deployed
in under 30 days and reconfigured across SKUs without a re-integration project.

Market Opportunity
The third-party logistics automation TAM is estimated at $48B by 2028,
growing at roughly 22% CAGR. Acme is positioned to capture the mid-market
3PL segment, which is currently underserved by enterprise-scale incumbents.

Go-to-market
Land-and-expand starting with single-warehouse pilots, expanding to fleet

Why Now
Labor scarcity, e-commerce fulfillment pressure, and rapid progress in
robotic foundation models are converging to make general-purpose warehouse
automation viable for the first time.
